Web and mobile application developer - graphic design expert

👋 Hello! I'm passionate about programming and design, convinced that you should never stop learning. I specialize in graphic design and web development, with experience in multiple full-stack projects, although my true passion is the front-end.

I consider myself a versatile developer, particularly fluent in mobile development, as I firmly believe that in Africa, our continent, mobile devices are the most accessible way to bring technology closer and improve our environment.

* 🌍  I'm based in Malabo, EQ

* 🧠  I'm learning Flutter, react native, PERN STACK
* 🤝  I'm open to collaborating on In any project where I can add value and have the opportunity to grow and learn more.
* ⚡  In my spare time, I love drawing, illustrating, creating animations, and 3D modeling. I'm proficient in graphic design tools (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), 3D (Blender 3D, AutoCAD), and video editing (Adobe Premier, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve).
